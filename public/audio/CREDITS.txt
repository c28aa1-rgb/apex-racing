Menu music: public/audio/music/ holds a 43-track playlist (playlist.json lists titles) of commercial game-soundtrack
and dance tracks the owner supplied for PERSONAL USE. They are NOT licensed for redistribution, so that folder is git-ignored
and must not be committed, deployed or shared. Without the folder the menu is simply silent.
