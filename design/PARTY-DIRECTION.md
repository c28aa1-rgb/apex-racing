# Shared Grid

The space before a race has its own precision. Parallel bays establish a measured rhythm; the machines interrupt it with individual form. Meticulously crafted intervals make each arrival legible without fragmenting the floor into containers. The empty spaces belong to the composition as much as the occupied ones.

Silver concrete and cloud-white lettering carry the broad surfaces. Coastal Circuit's harbor blue holds the controls, while seafoam identifies presence and racing orange marks ownership and commitment. Painstaking attention to contrast keeps the actual vehicles, their liveries and their silhouettes at the center of the work.

Condensed Big Shoulders gives names and places weight. Instrument Sans carries decisions at a quieter scale. Type belongs above its subject, never across it. The product of deep expertise is restraint: generous margins, short labels, and no ornamental explanation.

Motion marks a change in company. An arrival settles into a bay; a departure releases that space. A shared setting changes once, clearly, for everyone. Master-level execution means timing these responses as a single coherent gesture, with stillness restored immediately afterward.

## Interface Specification

- Theme: existing Coastal Circuit, with concrete #dce4e5, harbor #132b3b, seafoam #a8dadc, orange #ff784c, cloud #f1faee, ink #23343c.
- Type: existing Shoulders for headings at 32/48px; Instrument at 12/14/16px for controls and names. Letter spacing 0.
- Layout: full-bleed 3D parking paddock with actual cars and overhead names. Title and invitation code sit above the lineup; car selection and shared settings occupy one compact bottom bar. Four bays by default, expandable to eight. On mobile, bays form two columns and the bottom controls wrap.
- Motion: 120ms press feedback, 220ms selection, 360ms entrance; easing [0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1]. Reduced motion removes displacement and camera drift.
- Review: replaced generic player cards with named, actual vehicle models on one shared floor. Settings form one quiet bottom strip; empty bays indicate available places.
- Scope: lobby creation, code joining, presence, host ownership, cars and shared settings. No race start or multiplayer simulation.
