# Held Energy

Form expresses restrained mechanical power. A long, low body holds repeated circular apertures, while paired outboard rings make its weight feel suspended. Every joint should look meticulously crafted: not ornament, but evidence of something assembled with purpose.

Dark marine surfaces absorb light; brushed alloy catches it along narrow edges. A small warm accent identifies the working face. Color is functional and spare, the product of deep expertise rather than decoration.

Repetition establishes anticipation. Five identical stations progress in measured beats, each built from smaller points of light. Painstaking attention to spacing makes the interval between those beats as important as the lights themselves.

Empty space contains the motion. Balance is bilateral but never frozen: an almost imperceptible hover lends mass, and an upward departure releases the composition. Master-level execution lives in a restrained silhouette, credible fastening details, and the absence of visual noise.

## Interface plan

Retain the approved Coastal Circuit palette: harbor #132b3b, ocean #265466, seafoam #a8dadc, orange #ff784c, cloud #f1faee, sky #c4e1eb. Big Shoulders carries race identity; Instrument Sans handles controls and standings. Left-aligned labels and right-aligned tabular times suit a timing desk. Settings use grouped rows, not a grid of promotional cards. The drone is the single theatrical moment.

Settings: [Sound / Driving / Controls] [labeled controls and values]
Standings: [Circuit selector] [leader / personal benchmark] [rank, driver, time, gap, ghost]

Review: preserve the game's established maritime typography and palette. Replace eight cramped leaderboard tabs with a circuit selector; keep decorative motion out of settings. Progression exposes exact XP requirements and never obscures the next unlock.
